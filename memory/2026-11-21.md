**Verified:** pages work from `file://` in Chromium: self-hosted fonts load, no console

**Fact-check:** a research subagent checked 20 engine and citation claims against primary
docs. Corrections applied: Unity "Index Format" is in the Geometry section with "16 bit" /
"32 bit"; Unity Vertex Compression conditions; UE label "Allow CPUAccess"; UE picks 16/32-bit
indices per LOD automatically; Godot 4.2 compression wording (not "octahedral"); URP SSAO
label "Samples"; HDRP AO is GTAO only per its source, not its docs; Godot SSAO is
Forward+ (simplified in Compatibility since 4.6, not Mobile). Timings cited: GTAO 0.5 ms on
PS4 at 1080p; XeGTAO High 0.56 ms at 1080p on RTX 2060 and 1.4 ms at 4K on RTX 3070.
